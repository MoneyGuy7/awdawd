Welcome

Brunch Cafe is a casual spot on Lake Cook Road for breakfast, brunch and lunch. Come in for an omelet or a skillet, a stack of blueberry pancakes or a sandwich, and a good cup of coffee. We have a kids' menu, a full bar, and a free parking lot.

You can dine in, grab takeout or curbside pickup, or have it delivered. We also take reservations, which we recommend for weekend brunch.
