Good to know

Questions we get a lot
Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are accepted and recommended for brunch. Call (847) 607-9170.

Is there parking?

Yes. There's a free parking lot and free street parking, and there's usually plenty of room.

Is it wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The entrance, parking lot, restroom and seating are all wheelchair accessible.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes. Ask your server, and tell us about any allergies or dietary needs.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. We have a kids' menu and high chairs.

Do you serve alcohol?

Yes. We have an onsite bar with beer, wine, cocktails and hard liquor.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi for guests.

Takeout and delivery?

Yes: takeout, curbside pickup, delivery and no-contact delivery. Call us to order.

See you at breakfast

Casual, cozy and welcoming to everyone. LGBTQ+ friendly and a transgender safe space.
