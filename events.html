Showers, groups & catering

Planning a bridal shower or baby shower starts with choosing the right venue. A brunch celebration offers a relaxed atmosphere, delicious food, and a time of day that's convenient for guests of all ages.

How it works

  1. Call or send an inquiryTell us the date, the type of event and about how many guests.
  2. We confirm the detailsWe'll check availability and help with seating and the menu.
  3. Show up and celebrateFresh, made-to-order meals and friendly service. We handle the rest.

What's included

  • Fresh, made-to-order meals
  • Friendly, attentive service
  • Casual, cozy room
  • Full bar for toasts: beer, wine, cocktails
  • Kids' menu and high chairs for little guests
  • Wheelchair accessible entrance, seating and restrooms
  • Free parking lot for your guests
