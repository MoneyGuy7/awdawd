Showers, groups and catering

Planning a bridal shower or baby shower starts with choosing the right venue. A brunch celebration offers a relaxed atmosphere, delicious food, and a time of day that's convenient for guests of all ages.

Hosting with us

At Brunch Cafe, we make it easy to host memorable bridal showers and baby showers with fresh, made-to-order meals, friendly service, and a welcoming setting.

  • Full bar for toasts: beer, wine and cocktails
  • Kids' menu and high chairs for little guests
  • Wheelchair accessible entrance, seating and restrooms
  • Free parking lot for your guests
  • Reservations accepted

Catering and group brunches are available too. Call (847) 607-9170 or send a note and we'll get back to you.
